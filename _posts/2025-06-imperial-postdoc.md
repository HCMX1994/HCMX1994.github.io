In June 2025, I joined Imperial College London as a Postdoctoral Researcher, working with [Prof. Bruno Clerckx](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/b.clerckx) (IEEE Fellow).

My work focused on the design, prototyping and experimental characterisation of reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and metasurfaces, including beyond-diagonal reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (BD-RIS) for next-generation wireless communications.
